Learnings

What's actually
sticking.

01

Prompts are the new interface

How you talk to AI determines what you get back. Clarity, context, and constraints matter more than the model version. Most people skip this.

02

Ship early or not at all

The gap between "idea" and "thing that exists" is almost entirely about starting before you're ready. Waiting for perfect is just procrastination with better branding.

03

AI doesn't replace judgment

The more you use AI tools, the clearer it becomes: they accelerate execution, not thinking. Knowing what to build still matters more than knowing how.

04

Agents are still early

The frameworks are moving fast — LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen. But the fundamentals haven't changed: garbage in, garbage out. Context management is everything.

05

VC pattern recognition

Good investors don't just evaluate businesses — they read people. The founder-market fit question is usually more important than the market size question.

06

Learning in public works

Writing things down, building in public, sharing what you're figuring out — it compounds. You learn more, meet better people, and stay honest about what you don't know.
